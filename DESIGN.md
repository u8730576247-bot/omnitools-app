# KillKit Design System

> "We help you kill all your problems... or at least most of them."

---

# Philosophy

KillKit is not a launcher.

KillKit is an ecosystem.

Every application is a node.
Every node belongs to a larger structure.

The interface should communicate order, clarity and confidence.

---

# Core Principles

## Simplicity

Every visual element must have a purpose.

---

## Structure

The ecosystem grows like a crystal.

Not like a list.

---

## Motion

Animations reveal information.

They never distract.

---

## Color

Primary background:
- Deep Space

Primary accent:
- Electric Green = access, activation

Secondary accents:
- Blue
- White
- Neutral gray

---

# Fundamental Elements

## Node

Circular.
Concentric rings.
Always recognizable.

---

## Connection

Represents relationships between applications.

---

## Core

The central KillKit node.

---

## Pulse

Electric green animation indicating activity.
